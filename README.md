## 🏛️ What is AQREVIA?
**AQREVIA** is a modern real-estate technology platform uniting two essential pillars:
1. **The Real Estate Marketplace**: Consumer discovery of apartments, luxury villas, commercial tech parks, and residential plots with interactive filtering, floorplan matrices, direct owner/developer connects, and automated site visit scheduling.
2. **The Real Estate Growth Platform**: Enterprise digital infrastructure for developers, builders, brokers, and channel partners providing hyper-targeted performance marketing (Google & Meta), architectural 4K video walkthrough production, custom microsite engineering, and sub-60 second conversational AI lead qualification.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🏢 Real Estate Marketplace
- **Discovery Engine**: Search across prime Indian metros (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR) by locality, BHK, price brackets, and RERA status.
- **Rich Property Detail Pages**: High-resolution gallery, unit dimensions, carpet area, verified badges, amenity grids, and coordinate viewports.
- **Direct Interactive Connect**:
  - Direct Phone Call trigger
  - Click-to-WhatsApp direct routing
  - Inbound Enquiry form with automated CRM ingestion
  - Private Site Visit scheduling
  - Save & Compare (side-by-side comparison of up to 4 properties)
  - Inaccurate listing report audit modal

### 🏗️ Master-Planned Projects & Developers
- **Project Portals**: Detailed RERA verified profiles, master plans, e-brochure downloads, unit configuration matrix, and developer credentials.
- **Developer Directory**: Track records of delivered square footage, completed vs. ongoing projects.
- **Broker Directory**: Operational micro-markets, closed transaction counts, and active client listings.

### 📈 Growth Platform (Enterprise Suite)
- **Performance Marketing**: Calibrated Meta & Google search ad funnels for high-net-worth property buyers.
- **High-Converting Websites**: Sub-second microsites with interactive floor plans.
- **Architectural Video Production**: 4K walkthroughs, aerial drone surveys, and viral vertical short-form reels.
- **AI Lead Automation**: Sub-60s WhatsApp screening verifying buyer budget and auto-booking weekend site visits.
- **360° Project Launch Blitz**: Integrated launch strategy driving 100+ site visits in 30 days.

### 🛡️ Admin & CRM Pipeline
- **Listing Moderation**: Manage drafts, reviews, approvals, suspensions, and feature toggles.
- **Lead CRM Pipeline**: Full Kanban lifecycle (`NEW` → `CONTACTED` → `QUALIFIED` → `SITE_VISIT` → `WON`/`LOST`) with executive assignment and timestamped notes.
- **Site Visit Logistics**: Calendar schedules and executive hand-offs.
- **Security Audit Trails**: Real-time logging of administrative events and role changes.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack
- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 with bespoke architectural design system
- **Design Constitution**: Zero-pill metadata discipline, WCAG AA compliance, tabular numerals (`tabular-nums`), resilient SVG image fallbacks.
- **State Management**: Reactive DataStore with persistence layer and instant cold-boot seed catalog.
- **SEO Ready**: Schema.org JSON-LD structured data (`RealEstateAgent`), OpenGraph social cards, and clean HTML5 history routes.
